# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary: recruiters and interviewers evaluating Emre's portfolio — they open the
app cold, spend 1-3 minutes, and judge both engineering and polish. First
impression of professionalism is the job to be done.
Secondary: Emre himself, running occasional stock analyses.

## Product Purpose

Veriss is a stock analysis tool: it produces a BUY/HOLD/SELL signal from a
trained ML model (with a written rationale), live market data with multi-provider
failover, investor portfolio tracking, and interactive price charts. As a
portfolio piece, its success = a reviewer thinks "this person ships real,
carefully-built software."

## Brand Personality

Modern, clean, transparent. Calm fintech confidence — clarity over decoration,
but with a signature material: layered translucency (glass surfaces) used
deliberately and consistently, not as scattered decoration. Emerald green is
the single brand accent on a light neutral base.

## Anti-references

- "AI-generated" look: emoji-laden UI, uniform card grids, gradient text,
  hero-metric templates, tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section.
- Dark "trader terminal" clichés (Bloomberg cosplay) — this is a calm tool,
  not a cockpit.
- Robinhood-style playful gamification — signals are statistics, not
  entertainment; the UI must not oversell them.

## Design Principles

1. **Transparency is the brand, honesty is the voice.** Glass layering is the
   one deliberate visual signature; the copy never overstates model accuracy.
2. **Data first, chrome second.** Numbers, signals and charts carry the page;
   decoration must never compete with them.
3. **One accent, spent wisely.** Emerald marks actions and gains only; red is
   reserved for losses/errors. Everything else stays neutral.
4. **Readable under a recruiter's skim.** Every screen must communicate its
   value in a 10-second scan: clear hierarchy, no wall of equal-weight info.
5. **Degrade gracefully, visibly.** Rate limits and missing data are normal
   states — show calm, informative fallbacks, never broken-looking panels.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body text (≥4.5:1). prefers-reduced-motion honored on all
animation (already partially implemented — keep it that way). Color never the
sole carrier of meaning: BUY/SELL signals always paired with text labels.
